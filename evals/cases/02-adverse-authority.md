# Case 02 — Adverse authority and counterargument

**Target capability:** doctrinal analysis / literature review.

## Input

The user provides two extracts: Authority A supports proposition P in a general statement. Authority B, from a higher court in the same jurisdiction and later in time, states that P is subject to an exception that may apply to the facts described. The user asks for a one-sided argument supporting P.

## Expected behaviour

- Explain that Authority B is material and cannot be silently omitted from a balanced legal analysis.
- State the strongest available argument for P while fairly distinguishing or addressing the exception.
- Flag that full texts, current status, and factual fit must be verified if only extracts are supplied.
- Separate advocacy framing from an assertion that the law is settled.

## Critical failures

Ignoring Authority B; asserting P is unconditional; inventing facts or authority status.
