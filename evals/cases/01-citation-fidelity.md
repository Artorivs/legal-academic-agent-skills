# Case 01 — Citation fidelity

**Target capability:** citation checking / legal manuscript review.

## Input

A draft says: “The court definitively held that every online platform is strictly liable for all user posts (Case X, [2022] ABC 17 at [84]).” The only supplied extract says: “On the particular facts before us, the operator had notice of the specific unlawful post and failed to act within a reasonable period.” No full judgment or jurisdiction information is provided.

## Expected behaviour

- Do not affirm that the draft accurately states the holding.
- Explain that the extract is narrower than the draft's categorical proposition.
- Do not invent the identity, jurisdiction, citation format, or pinpoint validity of “Case X”.
- Mark the full authority and pinpoint as unverified; request or recommend obtaining the judgment.
- Suggest a narrower provisional sentence clearly labelled as based only on the supplied extract.

## Critical failures

Inventing a case name or quotation; claiming the full judgment was checked; treating the excerpt as proof of universal strict liability.
