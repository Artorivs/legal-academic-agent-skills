# Claim–evidence matrix (template)

| ID | Draft claim | Evidence/source | Exact pinpoint | Source type and authority | Support level | Contrary evidence / caveat | Follow-up |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| C-01 | [Proposition to support] | [Full citation or source identifier] | [Page/paragraph/section] | [Primary / secondary; jurisdiction; date] | [Direct / partial / contextual / unverified] | [Exception, limitation, contrary source] | [Verification action] |

## Instructions

- One row per material proposition; split compound claims.
- Never invent citations or pinpoints to complete a row.
- Use “unverified” when the source text has not been inspected.
- Record the version/date of legal sources where amendments or later treatment may matter.
- Keep analysis separate from what the source expressly says.
