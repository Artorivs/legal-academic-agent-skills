# Skill Index

- [`academic-project-router`](skills/academic-project-router/SKILL.md) — Choose and coordinate the appropriate academic legal research or writing workflow. Use when a task spans several kinds of work or the next step is unclear.
- [`research-planning`](skills/research-planning/SKILL.md) — Design a rigorous research plan for academic legal work, including research questions, scope, method, source strategy, and manageable research stages.
- [`source-verification`](skills/source-verification/SKILL.md) — Verify legal, academic, empirical, and archival sources and match each source to the precise proposition it supports. Use before relying on unfamiliar citations or adding authorities.
- [`legal-authority-analysis`](skills/legal-authority-analysis/SKILL.md) — Analyse cases, legislation, treaties, international legal materials, and administrative decisions with attention to authority, context, reasoning, limits, and competing readings.
- [`literature-synthesis`](skills/literature-synthesis/SKILL.md) — Synthesize legal and interdisciplinary scholarship into an analytical account of debates, methods, evidence, and contribution rather than a sequence of source summaries.
- [`argument-mapping`](skills/argument-mapping/SKILL.md) — Map an academic legal thesis into linked claims, evidence, warrants, assumptions, counterarguments, and vulnerabilities before drafting or revising.
- [`academic-legal-drafting`](skills/academic-legal-drafting/SKILL.md) — Turn notes, sources, outlines, or rough prose into connected academic legal writing with a clear claim, developed reasoning, and careful source use.
- [`adversarial-writing-cycle`](skills/adversarial-writing-cycle/SKILL.md) — Run a repeatable draft–critique–response–revision–retest cycle to expose weaknesses in an academic legal argument and record unresolved objections.
- [`critical-peer-review`](skills/critical-peer-review/SKILL.md) — Review a legal academic manuscript as a rigorous peer reviewer, examiner, or editor and identify consequential weaknesses with specific remedies.
- [`citation-audit`](skills/citation-audit/SKILL.md) — Audit footnotes, pinpoint citations, quotations, bibliographies, and source-to-claim fit in a legal academic manuscript.
- [`bilingual-legal-writing`](skills/bilingual-legal-writing/SKILL.md) — Draft, revise, or translate legal scholarship between English and Chinese while preserving doctrinal meaning, jurisdictional terminology, and register.
- [`project-continuity`](skills/project-continuity/SKILL.md) — Continue a long academic research project across sessions or models using stable records, explicit decisions, corrections, evidence gaps, and concise handovers.
- [`manuscript-consolidation`](skills/manuscript-consolidation/SKILL.md) — Consolidate a manuscript by removing repetition, improving structure and transitions, and preserving necessary evidence, qualifications, and argumentative steps.
- [`final-manuscript-audit`](skills/final-manuscript-audit/SKILL.md) — Perform the final substantive and editorial audit of an academic legal manuscript before submission or circulation.
- [`anticipatory-analysis`](skills/anticipatory-analysis/SKILL.md) — Create and apply a pre-specified protocol for analysing future events or incoming evidence without retrofitting the method to the outcome.
